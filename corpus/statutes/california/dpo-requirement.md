# California CCPA/CPRA: No general Data Protection Officer mandate
Retrieved: 2026-08-17

California has no general Data Protection Officer mandate under the CCPA/CPRA. Unlike GDPR, India DPDP, Brazil LGPD, and Singapore PDPA, California law does not require businesses to appoint a DPO. This is a structural gap in the jurisdiction, relevant to the project's sparse-cell eval bucket.
