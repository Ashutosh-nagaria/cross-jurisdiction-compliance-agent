**KlioHR Systems Global Privacy Organization**

**Overview**
To maintain compliance across the five distinct regulatory environments in which we operate, KlioHR Systems has established a globally distributed privacy organization. This structure ensures that regional nuances are respected while maintaining a unified corporate data governance strategy. The following details the key personnel, their reporting lines, and their jurisdictional responsibilities.

**Executive Leadership**

* **Fiona Gallagher, General Counsel:** Located in the Boston HQ, Fiona oversees all legal and compliance functions worldwide. The Global Privacy Office reports directly to her, ensuring independence from engineering and sales organizations.

**Global Privacy Office**

* **Marcus Vance, Chief Privacy Officer and Global DPO:** Also based in Boston, Marcus sets the strategic direction for privacy engineering, manages the global data inventory, and oversees the handling of multi-jurisdictional data breaches.

**Regional Privacy Leads**

* **Elena Rostova, EU Data Protection Officer:** Based in Dublin, Ireland.
* *Mandate:* Formally appointed under Article 37 of the GDPR due to our large scale processing of employee monitoring data. Elena acts as the primary liaison with the Irish Data Protection Commission and handles all privacy escalations originating from the European market.

* **Rahul Sharma, India Grievance Officer:** Based in Bangalore, India.
* *Mandate:* Appointed to fulfill the strict requirements of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. Rahul manages localized user consent records, responds to data principal requests within the required local timelines, and interacts with the Data Protection Board of India.

* **Camila Santos, Latin America Privacy Counsel:** Based in Sao Paulo, Brazil.
* *Mandate:* Appointed to align with the LGPD requirement for a person in charge of processing personal data (Encarregado). Camila ensures localized compliance for customer contracts and oversees localized employee data handling.

* **David Chen, APAC Privacy Lead:** Based in Singapore.
* *Mandate:* Ensures alignment with the Singapore PDPA. David reviews data flows across the Asian subsidiaries, audits localized marketing campaigns against the Do Not Call registry, and manages cross border data transfer agreements within the APAC region.

* **Sarah Jenkins, US Privacy Counsel:** Based in San Francisco, California.
* *Mandate:* Focuses on state level US privacy laws, primarily the CCPA/CPRA. Sarah ensures our software platform correctly supports opt-out mechanisms and manages vendor assessment processes for service providers interacting with Californian data.
