**Introduction**
This document outlines the primary categories of personal data collected, stored, and processed by KlioHR Systems. It serves as the foundation for our global privacy compliance program, mapping data assets to their respective data subjects and the key regulatory regions where KlioHR operates.

| Data Category | Specific Data Elements | Data Subjects | Applicable Regions |

**Regional Context and Processing Justifications**

* **European Union (GDPR):** Data relating to EU residents is processed strictly under the basis of our customer data processing agreements (Article 28). We act as the processor, while our customers remain the controllers.
* **California (CCPA/CPRA):** For California residents, data is classified under the CPRA framework. KlioHR acts as a Service Provider. We collect business contact information and employee metrics exclusively to provide the contracted HR software services.
* **India (DPDP Act):** Data processed in our Bangalore development center or on behalf of Indian customers falls under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act. We ensure processing is tied to lawful purposes established by our clients.
* **Brazil (LGPD):** Data concerning individuals in Brazil is managed under the LGPD. Recruitment and employee data processing relies on the execution of employment contracts and legitimate business interests defined by our clients.
* **Singapore (PDPA):** Processing of APAC user data routed through our Singapore headquarters complies with the Personal Data Protection Act, focusing heavily on explicit purpose limitation and reasonable security arrangements.
