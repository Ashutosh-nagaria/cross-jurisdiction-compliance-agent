**Overview**
KlioHR Systems provides an enterprise workforce analytics and talent management platform. Our software helps large organizations track employee engagement, measure productivity, manage performance reviews, and streamline candidate recruitment pipelines. Because our platform acts as the central hub for human resources operations, we process significant volumes of sensitive corporate and personal information on behalf of our clients.

* **United States:** Global Headquarters in Boston, MA, and a regional sales and marketing office in San Francisco, California.
* **European Union:** An administrative and customer success branch located in Dublin, Ireland, serving our EU customer base.

**Customer Base and Scale**
KlioHR currently serves approximately 1,400 enterprise and mid-market customers across the globe. Our platform actively manages profiles for over 3.5 million end-users, encompassing both current employees of our customers and historical job applicants.

**Personal Data Processed**
As a data processor for our B2B clients, and a data controller for our own staff and marketing operations, we handle the following categories of personal data:

* **Employee Data:** Names, corporate email addresses, job titles, department structures, compensation bands, performance review scores, peer feedback, and leave records.
* **Candidate Data:** Resumes, contact information, interview notes, assessment scores, and background check statuses.
* **Usage Analytics:** IP addresses, login timestamps, feature adoption metrics, device information, and geographic location data derived from corporate network configurations.
* **Administrative Data:** Billing details, contract signatures, and primary contact information for customer account administrators.
