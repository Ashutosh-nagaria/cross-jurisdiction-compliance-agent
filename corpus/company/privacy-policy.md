We use the collected data to provide, maintain, and improve our platform. KlioHR Systems does not sell your personal information. We protect these transfers using legally recognized mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers out of the European Economic Area, and intra company data transfer agreements to secure data flowing to our India and Singapore offices.
